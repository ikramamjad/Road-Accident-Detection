How the System Eliminates False Positives

Single-model detection fails in complex road conditions. Our multi-stage architecture verifies incident causality before raising an alert.

1 Detection & Tracking

YOLO11 identifies vehicles and pedestrians. BoT-SORT maintains temporal track consistency across occlusions and camera motion.

2 Kinematics & Pose

Time-to-Collision (TTC) computes closing speeds. Gated YOLO11-Pose measures torso fall angles and prolonged ground posture.

3 Causal GRU & Debounce

Strictly causal recurrent neural network models behavioral anomaly. A 6-frame debounce filter suppresses transient brake lights.
